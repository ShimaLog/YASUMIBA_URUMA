FAQ

How many guests can stay?

Up to 9 guests. For 6 or more guests, an extra charge of 2,000 yen per person applies (from the property's rules on Booking.com).

What beds are there?

2 single beds, 4 futons and 1 sofa bed, in 3 bedrooms.

Is there parking?

Free parking for 2 regular cars and up to 3 kei cars.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 4:00 to 10:00 pm. Check-out is by 10:00 am.

Is it good for families with children?

The kids' room has toys. A cot for ages 0–1 is free.

Can we cook?

The kitchen has a fridge, a microwave, and pots and pans.

How far is it from Naha Airport?

About 50–60 minutes by car, via the Okinawa Expressway (Okinawa-kita IC).

Are there shops and restaurants nearby?

Restaurants and a supermarket are within a 10-minute walk.

How far are Islands you can drive to?

Kaichu Road is 20 minutes by car. Hamahiga and Miyagi islands are 30 minutes, and Ikei Island is 40 minutes.

How do I book?

Book on Booking.com. Prices vary by season.

Does the host speak English?

Host communication is in Japanese.
